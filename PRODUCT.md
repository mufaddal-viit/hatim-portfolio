# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective interior-design clients — homeowners and small commercial owners deciding
whether to start a conversation about a renovation or fit-out. They arrive from
Instagram/Pinterest or a referral, browse on phone or desktop, and judge the studio by
how the site itself feels: if the site is beautiful and calm, the rooms will be too.

## Product Purpose

Portfolio and lead-generation site for an interior design studio. Success is a visitor
scrolling the work, trusting the taste level, and sending the first email ("Let's talk
about your space"). The site is the studio's primary proof of craft.

## Brand Personality

Warm, crafted, assured. Espresso and cream materials, serif display type used at
architectural scale, photography doing the persuading. Confident without being loud —
the voice of a studio that has done this 120 times and doesn't need to shout.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing-page grammar: gradient CTAs, icon card grids, hero-metric bento.
- Sterile gallery minimalism (white void + tiny gray captions) — reads cold, not warm.
- Template portfolio look with visible seams between sections; the site should read as
  one continuous composition, like a well-designed room.

## Design Principles

1. **Photography leads.** The imagery is the product; type and chrome frame it.
2. **Type as architecture.** The display serif breaks across boundaries (panel → photo)
   to weld the layout into one composition.
3. **Warm materiality.** Espresso surfaces, frosted glass, hairline light edges —
   surfaces feel like materials, not UI panels.
4. **Seamlessness.** Adjacent surfaces blend rather than butt; hard seams are a bug.
5. **Identity preservation.** Playfair Display and the espresso palette are committed
   brand choices; refinements build on them rather than replacing them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for all text over photography (scrims/shadows guarantee it).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured everywhere: no autoplay advance, no Ken Burns,
  crossfade-or-instant alternatives.
- Slideshow controls keyboard-reachable with visible focus; decorative echoes
  `aria-hidden`.
