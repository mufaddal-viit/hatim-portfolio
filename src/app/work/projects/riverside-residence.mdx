Open plan fails when it becomes one undifferentiated space. The zones here are
separated by level and material rather than by walls: a stone run for cooking, a
timber table that sits a step down, and a sitting area that keeps its back to the
kitchen.

The pale stone was chosen for how it takes river light in the morning, which is
flat and cool and unforgiving of anything with too much yellow in it.
