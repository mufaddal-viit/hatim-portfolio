Panelling is easy to get wrong. Set the rhythm too wide and it reads as cladding;
too narrow and the room feels ribbed. Here the bays are set out from the window
reveals, so the lines resolve against the openings instead of fighting them.

Everything else stays quiet: linen, a low lamp, and a floor left bare enough that
the oak has room to be the only warm thing in the room.
