The brief was short: keep the light, lose the corridor. The original plan pushed
every room off a long central hallway, which left the best of the afternoon sun
sitting in circulation space nobody used.

Removing two non-structural walls turned three rooms into one living space that
runs the full depth of the floor. The gallery wall came last — the owners had
been collecting for years without anywhere to hang it.
